A little about me

Hi, I’m Jay! I’m a computer science student graduating in December 2026, and I’m working toward a career in software engineering. I’m also taking a full stack development course to get more hands-on experience building both front-end and back-end applications.

Before getting into tech, I built my career in pharmacy. Working in a fast-paced environment taught me how to prioritize, think on my feet, pay attention to details, and help people with their individual needs. Those are skills I bring with me when I’m solving problems and building projects.

So far, I’ve learned the foundations of Java, Python, and C++, along with SQL, MongoDB, HTML, and CSS. Right now, I’m focused on getting more comfortable with JavaScript and putting what I learn into practice.

Outside of coding, I love traveling and trying new food. I’ve visited eight countries so far, and I’m always excited to experience another culture through its food. I speak English and Spanish, and I’m conversational in Portuguese, so I’m getting close to trilingual!
